fix(matrix): retract the overstated scheduling gain; add a real RED

Independent review reproduced two HIGH defects in the previous commit. Both
are confirmed here by re-derivation, and both are corrections of my own work.

1. The headline scheduling gain was an artifact of a hidden denominator.
   The comparison receipts scored partitions on tables covering only 24/41
   (run 1) and 29/41 (run 2) cases, pricing every un-run case at the constant
   DEFAULT_COST_SECONDS. Proof: the group loads sum to exactly 34.0s and 24.0s
   more than the measured totals - 17*2.0 and 12*2.0 - and the missing set is
   exactly the NOT_RUN starvation tail, i.e. the heavy end. On the only
   full-coverage (41/41) table the real critical-path gain is 1.5226s / 2.02%
   (75.5306s -> 74.0080s against a 67.6663s bound), and 79.8% of random 20/21
   partitions beat the old rule. The 16-21s figures are withdrawn.

   The comparison script now REFUSES any table below 41/41 coverage and says
   why, the two partial-coverage receipts are deleted, and the README, handoff
   and findings state the true size and explicitly deny near-optimality.

2. The static cost model is now the plain run-count model, not the per-layer
   one. The per-layer variant looked near-perfect on the W07 table (ratio
   1.0008) but is overfit: it has only 4 distinct values across 41 cases, its
   optimal weight region is a narrow plateau, its Spearman correlation with
   real cost (0.7855) is below run-count's (0.8247), and under 5000 bootstrap
   resamples it degrades to a mean ratio of 1.0598 and beats the old rule in
   only 64.2% of draws - worse than run-count's 1.0498 / 70.2%. The rejection
   and its numbers are recorded in the module and pinned by a test.

   static_cost now also honours its cost_of hook, so a caller holding measured
   per-case seconds reaches the bound (ratio 1.0000). The runner does not use
   it implicitly, so a batch never depends on a prior run's timing.

3. The A-node RED was largely tautological: on pristine base e8a8558, 6 of the
   8 failures were ModuleNotFoundError and 1 was FileNotFoundError, leaving a
   single behavioural assertion. The position-round-robin defect had no
   behavioural RED; its "negative control" only re-implemented the old rule and
   passed on base. Three tests now drive the real cli_main.main and fail on the
   genuine defect - cli_main never consulted a scheduling owner - plus the
   missing report record and the unnamed batch stop.

Also corrected: implementation_commits is no longer empty, changed_paths is
complete, the 32-pass and 339-pass results have separate receipts, the recovery
script's own exit 0 is no longer written as if a real batch had exited 0, all
three real batches are listed including the weakest, and the supplier_calls /
production_writes claims now carry an explicit limitation because the C-run
restore artifacts were deleted with .tmp/.

No limits were changed, no case weakened, no denominator trimmed, and no
41 PASS is claimed.
